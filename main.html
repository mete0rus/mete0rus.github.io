Alchemical Correspondences

Glyph Body Metaphysical Prospect
☉ Sol Consciousness, identity, vital force. The seat of the Self — governs will, purpose, and the animating spark behind all intention.
☽ Luna Emotion, intuition, the subconscious. Rules dreams, cycles, memory, and the receptive mind. Bridges the waking self to the deep current beneath it.
☿ Mercury Communication, intellect, the messenger between worlds. Commands language, travel, duality, and the shaping of thought into form.
♀ Venus Love, beauty, magnetic attraction. Rules harmony, value, desire, and the force that draws things together. The principle of union.
♁ Earth Grounding, manifestation, the material plane. Anchors intention into physical reality. The crossroads of all forces meeting in matter.
♂ Mars Will, action, drive, and primal force. Commands courage, conflict, initiation. The force that cuts through resistance to make things happen.
♃ Jupiter Expansion, abundance, wisdom, and divine law. Governs growth, fortune, sovereignty, and the broadening of what is possible.
♄ Saturn Discipline, time, karma, and limitation as teacher. Commands structure, binding, shadow work, and transformation earned through endurance.
♅ Uranus Revolution, sudden awakening, and the higher mind. Breaks fixed patterns. Rules invention, liberation, and insight that arrives like lightning.
♆ Neptune Mysticism, dissolution, transcendence. Governs the veil, illusion, the deep unconscious, and the merging of self with something greater.
